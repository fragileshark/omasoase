*Compiled February 19, 2026*

## 1. Competitive Landscape

### Executive Summary

The German care home directory/comparison market is **fragmented and underdeveloped**. There is no equivalent of A Place For Mom (US) or Lottie (UK). The market is split between government/insurance portals (data-rich, terrible UX), commercial lead-gen directories (decent UX, sparse data), and a few care-tech startups focused on adjacent problems (home care, hospital discharge) rather than consumer-facing care home comparison.

---

### Government / Insurance Portals

#### AOK Pflegenavigator
- **URL**: https://www.aok.de/pk/pflegenavigator/
- **Offer**: Search for care homes, ambulatory services, day care, counseling centers. Official quality inspection results (24 quality aspects from MD inspections). Price data (Eigenanteil breakdown).
- **Coverage**: National — 30,000+ facilities
- **Data quality**: Best official data source in Germany. 95% of MD inspection results.
- **Business model**: Free public service funded by AOK (Germany's largest health insurer)
- **UX**: Functional but clinical government-portal feel. No user reviews, no photos.
- **Note**: Absorbed the Weisse Liste (weisse-liste-pflege.de), which was an independent quality portal by Bertelsmann Foundation — discontinued October 2024, redirects to AOK Pflegenavigator.
- **Threat**: Low. Authoritative data but poor UX, not designed for the emotional family decision context.

#### vdek Pflegelotse
- **URL**: https://www.pflegelotse.de/
- **Offer**: National search for ~16,000 ambulatory services and ~15,000 care homes. Quality inspection results and price/service data.
- **Coverage**: National
- **Data quality**: Updated twice weekly (addresses, structure, prices). Quality data updated daily.
- **Business model**: Free public service run by vdek (Verband der Ersatzkassen — TK, Barmer, DAK, etc.)
- **Threat**: Low. Same issue — authoritative data, terrible consumer experience.

#### BKK PflegeFinder
- **URL**: https://pflegefinder.bkk-dachverband.de/
- **Offer**: Search for ambulatory, day/night care, care homes, short-term care
- **Coverage**: National
- **Business model**: Free, run by BKK (company health insurance) umbrella
- **Threat**: Very low.

#### Heimfinder NRW (State Government)
- **URL**: https://www.mags.nrw/pflegeplatzsuche-mit-dem-heimfinder-nrw
- **Offer**: **Real-time availability** of care home beds in NRW. Care homes legally required to report daily availability.
- **Coverage**: NRW only
- **Business model**: Free, government-mandated
- **Key insight**: Only German portal with **mandatory real-time availability reporting**. Shows what is possible if governments mandate transparency.

#### Pflegefinder Bayern
- **URL**: https://www.stmgp.bayern.de/pflege/pflegefinder/
- **Offer**: Care facility search for Bavaria, including availability data
- **Coverage**: Bavaria only

#### AVACANO (Baden-Wurttemberg)
- **URL**: https://www.avacano.de/
- **Offer**: Daily updated availability data for care facilities.
- **Coverage**: Regional — BW (Baden-Wurttemberg), supported by state government
- **UX**: Functional, district-level filtering
- **Key fact**: In OmasOase's target market (Stuttgart/BW). But B2B-oriented (hospital discharge coordinators), lacks consumer-friendly comparison, reviews, and pricing.
- **Threat**: Moderate if they expand. But not consumer-facing.

#### Compass Private Pflegeberatung
- **URL**: https://www.compass-pflegeberatung.de/
- **Offer**: Counseling for privately insured persons. Care facility search. Phone/video/home visit consultations.
- **Coverage**: National. Funded by private health insurance (PKV).
- **Threat**: Low. Counseling-focused, not comparison-focused.

### Major Commercial Platforms

#### pflege.de (acquired by Paul Hartmann AG)
- **URL**: https://www.pflege.de/
- **Offer**: Germany's largest digital care portal. Comprehensive ratgeber (guide) content. Care home search + information. Care aid supply (curabox), stairlift/bathroom renovation leads.
- **Business model**: Lead generation + product referrals. Acquired by Paul Hartmann AG (medical/hygiene company) in January 2021.
- **Traffic**: ~1.4 million monthly visits (12 million annual, company claim)
- **Team**: 100+ employees (Hamburg)
- **Data quality for care homes**: Informational content is excellent. Care home search itself is more guide than comparison — no inline pricing, no user reviews on listings.
- **Threat**: HIGH for SEO competition. Dominates organic search for care-related keywords. But their care home comparison UX is mediocre — primarily a content/lead-gen machine.

#### Verbund Pflegehilfe (pflegehilfe.org)
- **URL**: https://www.pflegehilfe.org/
- **Offer**: Germany's largest care counseling service. 130 phone advisors. Free counseling + referral to care providers.
- **Business model**: Lead generation / referral fees. Free for consumers. ~200+ employees.
- **Coverage**: National. Established 2008.
- **Scale**: 1.3 million+ counseling conversations. TUV-certified "very good." Revenue ~$11.6M annually (estimate).
- **UX**: Phone-first model. Website has care home listings by city but sparse data.
- **Threat**: Moderate. Own the phone counseling channel but weak digital/self-service comparison.

#### Wohnen im Alter
- **URL**: https://www.wohnen-im-alter.de/
- **Offer**: Directory of 30,000+ care providers. Search for care homes, assisted living, day care. Some price data inline ("mtl. Eigenanteil: ab X EUR"). Availability status ("Freie Platze: Ja/Nein/Auf Anfrage").
- **Business model**: Provider profiles (freemium SaaS). Providers pay for premium profile placement. Also revenue from aid product comparisons (stairlifts, mobility).
- **Traffic**: ~332,000 monthly visits. 350,000+ inquiries per year.
- **Team**: Karlsruhe-based.
- **Data quality**: Best among commercial platforms. Shows partial pricing, availability, facility types. But no ratings/reviews. Pricing only shown for some facilities.
- **Threat**: MODERATE-HIGH for feature overlap. Closest national competitor to OmasOase model. But lacks user reviews, Google ratings, detailed per-PG pricing breakdown.

#### Deutsches Seniorenportal
- **URL**: https://www.seniorenportal.de/
- **Offer**: Germany's largest senior living portal/media network. Directory of care homes, assisted living, ambulatory services.
- **Business model**: Provider listings (paid profiles). Media network. Certification program.
- **Traffic**: Claims up to 5.2 million clicks per month.
- **Data quality**: Basic — name, address, distance, facility type. No pricing, no reviews, no photos in search results.
- **Threat**: Low-moderate. High traffic but poor data depth.

#### ProAgeMedia (altenheime.de + seniorenplatz-vermittlung.de)
- **URL**: https://www.altenheime.de/ / https://www.seniorenplatz-vermittlung.de/
- **Offer**: altenheime.de = care home directory (15,000+ listings, free hotline). seniorenplatz-vermittlung.de = active placement service with human advisors.
- **Business model**: Commission-based placement. Free for families. Care homes pay success-based fees.
- **Traffic**: 50,000+ monthly visitors (altenheime.de).
- **Data quality**: 91 care homes for Stuttgart. Name, address, facility type only. No pricing, no reviews, no photos, no availability.
- **Threat**: Low. Minimal data, phone-based model.

### Dead Player (Notable)

#### Seniorplace.de — SHUT DOWN Dec 31, 2023
- Was the closest German equivalent to A Place For Mom
- 24/7 free phone-based care home placement, nationwide
- Founded 2003 by Nicole Bowning — ran for almost 20 years
- **Commission-based model**: care homes paid when beds were filled
- **Significance**: The fact that the closest APFM equivalent in Germany died suggests commission-based placement may not be sustainable in Germany at scale.

### Review / Rating Platforms

#### werpflegtwie.de
- **URL**: https://werpflegtwie.de/
- Germany's only dedicated care facility review portal. Star ratings across 5 categories.
- Freemium for providers (90-day free, then paid to respond to reviews).
- Low adoption, sparse reviews. Concept is right but execution lacks scale.

#### Heimverzeichnis.de
- **URL**: https://heimverzeichnis.de/
- Directory of ~13,000 facilities with "Gruner Haken" (Green Check) quality-of-life certification.
- Non-profit / certification-focused. Not a consumer comparison tool.

## 2. OmasOase's Competitive Edge

### What nobody does well

| What families need | Who provides it | Quality |
|---|---|---|
| "Is there a bed available?" | Nobody in BW (AVACANO is B2B) | **Wide gap** |
| "What will I actually pay?" | AOK (terrible UX), wohnen-im-alter (partial) | **Weak** |
| "Is this place any good?" | Google (scattered), AOK (clinical reports) | **Fragmented** |
| All three, in one place | **Nobody** | **Wide open** |

### OmasOase's data advantage

OmasOase has 246 facilities for Stuttgart with **64 columns of enriched data** (AOK pricing + Google ratings/reviews + LLM-extracted website data). Competitors show 72-91 listings for Stuttgart with 5-10 fields.

### Where edge compounds

1. **Availability data (biggest moat)**: AI agent calling/WhatsApp-ing 246 facilities weekly. Proprietary, operationally intensive, hard to replicate at scale.
2. **Price transparency**: Per-Pflegegrad Eigenanteil in consumer-friendly UI. Nobody else does this.
3. **Google ratings integration**: marta's 2023 study proved massive interest, but no portal integrates them.
4. **Regional depth > national breadth**: National platforms won't invest city-by-city. Regional players can own the data.

### Where NOT to compete

- SEO for generic terms (pflege.de has 100+ employees and years of content)
- National coverage (wohnen-im-alter.de has 30K listings — spreading thin destroys depth)
- Phone-based counseling (Pflegehilfe has 130 advisors)

## 3. Recare — Deep Dive

### What it is

Berlin-based B2B health-tech SaaS (founded 2017). Digital marketplace for **hospital discharge management** — transferring patients from hospitals to aftercare providers (nursing homes, rehab, home care). Replaces the old process of faxing forms.

### How it works

1. Hospital social worker enters patient requirements into Recare
2. Platform auto-matches with available aftercare providers
3. Providers receive digital requests, accept/decline
4. Secure communication replaces fax/phone
5. Documentation automated

### Scale

- ~700 acute hospitals (~2/3 of all German hospitals)
- 650+ rehab clinics
- 26,000 nursing care, homecare providers, and medical supply companies
- 300,000+ patients processed
- ~132 employees

### Funding

| Round | Date | Amount | Lead |
|-------|------|--------|------|
| Early rounds | 2017-2022 | ~$10M | Various |
| Growth | April 2024 | €3.2M | Existing + Gilion |
| Growth | January 2026 | **€37M** (incl. €7M option) | **DNV** (became largest shareholder) |
| **Total** | | **~€50-55M** | |

### Pflegeplatzmanager acquisition

- July 2023: Acquired main competitor Pflegeplatzmanager (share swap)
- Combined network: 650 hospitals, 550 rehab, 23,000 aftercare providers
- Pflegeplatzmanager dissolved Jan 1, 2024; all customers migrated
- Result: Near-monopoly in German digital discharge management

### Threat to OmasOase

**None.** Purely B2B — families never interact with Recare. Their DNA, tech stack, investors (DNV), and roadmap are all enterprise healthcare. Consumer marketplaces require completely different skills (SEO, brand, UX, trust). Low risk of pivot.

## 4. Market Sizing

### Germany vs UK vs US

| | Germany | UK | US |
|---|---|---|---|
| Care facilities | ~16,500 | ~17,000 | ~48,000 |
| Residents | ~908,500 | ~350-400K | ~2M |
| **Annual placements** | **~435,000** | **~170,000** | **~1.5-2M** |
| Avg monthly out-of-pocket | €3,100 | £5,200-6,600 | $9,300-11,300 |
| Market leader | Nobody | Lottie ($15M rev) | APFM ($623M rev) |

Germany has **2.5x the annual placements of the UK** and no consumer marketplace.

### Revenue ceiling for a German market leader

| Scenario | Model | Annual Revenue |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative (carehome.co.uk equivalent) | Subscriptions + ads | €10-25M |
| Medium (Lottie equivalent at maturity) | Lead fees + CRM SaaS + data | €30-60M |
| Aggressive (APFM-lite) | €500-1,500 per placement | €80-150M |

**Realistic 5-year target: €5-15M ARR**, with path to €30-50M via adjacent products (CRM, employer benefits, data API).

### Demographic tailwinds

| Metric | 2025 | 2035 | 2050 |
|--------|------|------|------|
| Population 80+ | 6.1M | ~7M | **8.5-9.8M** |
| Pflegebedurftige (total) | ~5.7M | ~6.3M | ~7.0M |
| In residential care | ~908K | ~1.0-1.1M | ~1.2-1.4M |
| Annual placements | ~435K | ~500K | ~550-700K |

The baby boomer generation (born 1955-1969) enters the 80+ bracket in the mid-2030s. Building now means being established before the wave hits.

## 5. Business Models — How the Big Players Make Money

### A Place For Mom (US) — Commission per placement

- **Free for families.** Care homes pay **90-120% of first month's rent** (~$3,500-7,000) per move-in.
- Only recommends facilities that pay them — systemic bias.
- Revenue: ~$623M/year, ~1,800 employees, valued at $1B+.
- **June 2024 US Senate investigation**: deceptive marketing, upselling, excluding low-cost options.
- 2025: Missouri bill requiring disclosure of financial ties.

### Lottie (UK) — Hybrid marketplace + SaaS

- **Pay-per-lead**: £30-60 per qualified enquiry
- **SaaS CRM** ("Found"): £150/month — occupancy management, enquiry tracking
- **Pay-per-customer**: charged only on actual move-in (for CRM users)
- **B2B employer benefit**: eldercare support sold to companies
- Total funding: ~$31M (Accel, General Catalyst). Revenue: ~$15M. 1M+ monthly users.
- Strategy: embed into care home operations via CRM, then take percentage of bookings (Booking.com model).

### carehome.co.uk (UK) — Pure subscription

- **Tiered listing fees** (Enhanced / Premium / Platinum). No referral fees.
- Dominates with **92% of all UK care directory traffic** (16M visits/year, 140K+ reviews).
- ~131 employees.
- Proves subscription model works if you own the traffic and reviews.

### Caring.com (US) — Hybrid

- Referral fees per move-in (~1 month's rent) + listing subscriptions + pay-per-lead (~$50) + advertising.
- Smaller than APFM but strong SEO and review database.

### What works for Germany?

Commission-per-placement is harder here because:
- 55% of homes are non-profit — culturally resistant to €2-3K referral fees
- Many homes at capacity or turning away patients (staff shortage)
- Proving attribution is difficult
- Seniorplace (closest APFM model) shut down after 20 years

**Recommended phasing:**

| Phase | Model | When |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (now) | Free. Validate demand with Google Ads. | Feb-Mar 2026 |
| 1 | Per-lead fees: €30-80 per qualified enquiry | Once traffic proven |
| 2 | Tiered subscriptions: €99-249/month for premium profiles | Once regional authority established |
| 3 | Availability data as B2B product (hospitals, Pflegestutzpunkte) | Once AI outreach operational |
| Adjacent | Care aid leads (Pflegehilfsmittel, Treppenlift, 24h-Betreuung) | Anytime |

## 6. Questions for Rheinland-Pfalz Contact (Feb 20, 2026)

### How they fill beds today

1. How do new residents typically find your facility? (Pflegestutzpunkt referral? Hospital discharge? Word of mouth? Online search?)
2. What percentage of your new admissions come through each channel?
3. Do you work with any referral services or directories currently? If so, which ones?
4. Do you list your facility on any online platforms? (AOK Pflegenavigator, wohnen-im-alter.de, pflege.de, etc.)

### Availability and occupancy

5. What is your current occupancy rate? Is it typical for your region?
6. Do you have a waitlist? If so, how long is the typical wait?
7. How often does bed availability change? (Daily? Weekly? Monthly?)
8. Would you be willing to share availability updates weekly via WhatsApp or a simple form?
9. Is staff shortage limiting your bed capacity? (i.e., do you have empty beds you can't fill because of staffing?)

### Pricing and transparency

10. Are families generally aware of the Eigenanteil before they contact you, or is pricing a surprise?
11. How do families react to the pricing when they first learn about it?
12. Do you think price transparency online helps or hurts you? (Does it drive away families or save everyone time?)
13. How often do your prices change? What drives the changes?

### Willingness to pay for leads

14. If a platform sent you qualified enquiries from families actively searching for a care home in your area, would that be valuable to you?
15. What would a qualified lead be worth to you? (€30? €50? €100? More?)
16. Would you prefer paying per lead, per move-in, or a monthly subscription for visibility?
17. What would make you NOT want to work with such a platform?

### Decision-making process (from the facility side)

18. When a family contacts you, what does the intake process look like?
19. What information do families typically ask for first? (Price? Availability? Quality? Location?)
20. What are the most common reasons families choose your facility over others?
21. What are the most common reasons families choose a competitor instead?

### Technology and operations

22. What software do you use for managing occupancy, enquiries, and resident data?
23. Would a CRM tool that tracks enquiries and manages your online profile be useful?
24. Do you have a website? How important is it for attracting new residents?

### Market perception

25. Are you aware of any online platforms that families in your region use to compare care homes?
26. What do you think is missing in the market for helping families find the right home?
27. If you could change one thing about how families find care homes, what would it be?

## 7. Pricing Accuracy Issue (Identified Feb 19, 2026)

### Problem

OmasOase displays `ownShare` (pgX_eigenanteil) from the AOK API, but the correct field is `personalContribution` (eigenanteil_display). This understates prices by ~25% vs what AOK itself shows.

### Cross-reference example: Ludwigstift

| Source | Amount | What it represents |
|---|---|---|
| OmasOase (current) | €2,515/mo | `ownShare` — excludes invest + training |
| AOK Pflegenavigator | €3,290/mo | `personalContribution` — official Eigenanteil |
| Actual first-year cost | ~€3,667/mo | Full out-of-pocket incl. invest + training + Leistungszuschlag |

### What's missing from ALL sources

- **Investitionskosten**: ~€300-500/mo (not in AOK API, varies by facility)
- **Ausbildungsumlage**: ~€100-200/mo (not in API, not mentioned in current disclaimer)
- **Leistungszuschlag**: Progressive discount (15% year 1, up to 75% year 4+) — partially offsets

### Fix

Switch display from `pgX_eigenanteil` to `eigenanteil_display`. Update disclaimer to mention Ausbildungsumlage.
